### What It Does
**Self-service energy audit tool** that analyzes commercial buildings and generates comprehensive energy savings recommendations in **10 minutes**.

### How It Works
1. **User Input** → Building details + utility bills (optional)
2. **AI Analysis** → Benchmark against industry standards + identify waste
3. **Instant Results** → Detailed report with actionable savings opportunities

### Target Users
- Business owners seeking energy cost reduction
- Property managers optimizing building performance
- Contractors identifying upgrade opportunities
- Energy auditors conducting preliminary assessments
